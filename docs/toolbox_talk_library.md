# Toolbox talk library

Use these short talks as editable starting points. Adapt them to the site's actual procedures and conditions, and check current controls with workers before delivery. A discussion prompt supports conversation; it is not a competency test.

## Stable loads: pause, check, and reset

**Trigger:** Near miss involving a pallet that shifted during a lift.

**Briefing:** Check the pallet and load before lifting, including damaged boards and incompatible or mixed footprints. Use the site's safe inspection method; do not put hands or feet under a suspended or unsupported load. Keep people outside the marked exclusion zone. If the load shifts or looks unstable, stop, lower it when safe, isolate the area, and report the condition. Quarantine suspect pallets according to site procedure.

**Crew discussion:** What pallet conditions would make you pause? How do you safely inspect an underside defect? Who should be notified if the pallet cannot be rejected or isolated through the normal process?

**Record:** Date, facilitator, attendees, and one understanding-check prompt. Attendance documents presence only and does not establish demonstrated competency.

## Vehicle and pedestrian separation

**Trigger:** Vehicle near miss, restricted visibility, or a change to a traffic route.

**Briefing:** Use marked pedestrian routes and crossing points. Make eye contact only where site procedure requires it; never assume a driver has seen you. Operators follow local speed, reversing, and visibility controls. Pause work and report blocked sightlines, damaged barriers, or route changes.

**Crew discussion:** Where are the blind spots on today's route? What is the site's safe response when separation is lost?

## Stop, isolate, and report

**Trigger:** Unexpected equipment movement, damage, leak, or changing work condition.

**Briefing:** Stop the task when a control is missing or conditions change. Use the approved isolation and emergency process for the hazard; do not approach an unknown release or unstable equipment. Warn others, establish a safe boundary, notify the responsible person, and preserve useful information when safe.

**Crew discussion:** Who can stop this task, and how do we restart only after the hazard is controlled?
